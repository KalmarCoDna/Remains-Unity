Remains patch, 2026-11-07:
Runtime/BatchedDeformation/GpuDeformationSystem.cs registers the existing
ClearFallbackBuffer callback with Application.quitting. Registration is idempotent;
normal Cleanup also removes the callback. No animation, skinning, shader, API,
resource, or package dependency version is changed.

The upstream AfterSceneLoad initializer creates s_FallbackBuffer even when no
SpriteSkin ever creates a DeformationManager. In that case its normal Cleanup
has no owner, and the Player finalizer reports an undisposed ComputeBuffer.

Regression: Remains.Editor.AnimationBufferLifetimeChecks.Run. It checks the
registered quit callback, actual buffer invalidation, repeated initialization,
and repeated cleanup. Player validation must also check final shutdown logs.

When replacing this embedded package, retain this ownership fix or verify that
the upstream package releases the fallback when no SpriteSkin is instantiated.
